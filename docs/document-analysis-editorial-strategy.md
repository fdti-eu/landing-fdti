Evidence inspected on September 19, 2026. Release notes document deployments; they are not a live production audit. ## Editorial architecture

1. Hub: positioning, six operations, links to three guides, anonymous court proof, limits and concrete scoping CTA.
2. Judicial and accounting records: pre-analysis and annual-accounts checks; show missing/unreadable/inconsistent input and human review.
3. Knowledge and watch: patent qualification, regulatory summaries and taxonomy classification. Keep historical prototype/internal project/adaptation labels visible.
4. Extraction and integration: text versus OCR, structured fields, review states and SI boundaries. No real documents or fabricated performance metrics.

Each experience needs an input, an operation, a usable output and a meaningful exception. Explanatory examples are not screenshots or production demonstrations. Source-backed summarization is a design objective; a model-generated citation is not a correctness guarantee.
